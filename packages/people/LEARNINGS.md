# LEARNINGS — packages/people/

- **Commute station zones are private.** Reference them only as `!secret commute_{home,midway,city}_station_zone` and keep file names, ids and aliases generic. Speak the real name at runtime with `trigger.zone.attributes.friendly_name`. `tests/test_commute_regressions.py` enforces this.
- **Don't match a person's state against a zone's friendly name.** That puts the name in the repo. Use `condition: zone` (it takes a list and ORs it). To keep a "held for N minutes" trigger, use `trigger: state` with `to: ~` plus `for:`, then check the zone in the conditions.
- **The turn_on/turn_off chains target alias-derived entity ids**, e.g. `automation.john_arrived_at_home_station`, not the `id`. Renaming an alias breaks the chain, and the test catches it.
- **A helper with no `initial:` reads its minimum, so "unset" needs its own value (medication stock, 2026-10-30).** `input_number.nino_medication_stock` has `min: -1`; the days sensor is `unavailable` below 0, so an unseeded count is silent instead of looking like "0 left". Guard writes that would turn -1 into 0: the refill and set scripts stop when days is not above 0, and the decrement needs stock above 0.
- **Fixed push ids outlive the push, so gate the handler on the live condition (JK-1259, 2026-11-15).** `medication_refill_push_action.yaml` acts only while the days sensor is known and under the threshold (the alert's own `low` test, pinned by a parity test). A tap on an old refill push after a refill does nothing instead of adding days again. Reload: automations.
- **Decrement with `input_number.decrement`, not read-then-`set_value`.** It is atomic and clamps at min; pair it with a `numeric_state above: 0` condition because min is -1 here. Trigger only `from: "off"` `to: "on"` so the 03:00 reset and restart restores never count.
- **Only core helper reloads are needed.** `input_number.reload` and `template.reload` exist, so new helpers do not need a restart.
- **Home presence no longer uses iPhone Detect or nmap (2026-11-07).** HA runs on the Servers VLAN, away from the phones. The MQTT tracker on the phones' VLAN publishes the same `device_tracker.*_iphone_detect` ids, so persons and Bayesian sensors needed no change; nmap is commented out of the Bayesian inputs. Tracker state isn't retained, so it reads `unknown` for about 12 s after a restart.
- **Departure announcements hold 1 minute (JK-1242).** A GPS flap at the zone edge (leave → enter → leave within 27 s) announced a departure twice. Every `*_announce_departure` trigger now has `for: minutes: 1` (and an explicit `to:`). Only the announcement waits; the away/home state, `Just Left` and house occupancy are not delayed. `tests/test_departure_debounce.py` covers it.
- **UniFi trackers blip home on a WLAN toggle (2026-11-11).** Switching the kids WLAN off briefly marked four offline clients home for about 9 minutes, Katia's phone among them. `katia_is_home` now requires `device_tracker.katia_iphone_detect` = home, which fails closed: if the MQTT tracker is down, she is never marked home, and the BLE `device_tracker.see` assist can't help.
- **A UniFi blip drives person.* directly, so gate every consumer, not just the home boolean (JK-1251, 2026-11-13).** Tayta's UniFi tracker flipped home 70 min after she left while `sonia_iphone_detect` stayed away; the arrival announcement, guest mode and the garage unlock all trigger on `person.sonia_koht`, not on `input_boolean.tayta_home`. The parents' arrival announcements and the garage unlock now wait `for: 1 minute` and require their detect tracker (detect trails UniFi by 4-20 s on a real arrival). Cleaner root fix, if wanted: drop the UniFi tracker from the parents' persons (UI config).
- **A room-presence template going `unavailable` is a state change.** `template.reload` flipped `sensor.tayta_room_presence` not_home -> unavailable and `from: not_home` fired `tayta_is_home`. Use `not_to: [unavailable, unknown]` on state triggers and `not in ['not_home','unavailable','unknown']` in templates (a bare `!= 'not_home'` is true for unavailable).

- **A person in no group is invisible to `group.all_people` (2026-11-14).** Joe was dropped from every group in the July 2025 refactor (he had been in `guests`), so when the last grouped person left with him home, `house_not_occupied` turned `house_occupied` off and the house went Away. He is back in `extended_family` (John's call: where he counted before the refactor), which nests into `guests` and `all_people`. That means `guest_mode` follows him: `guest_mode_on` counts him only once his iPhone detect agrees (like the parents), and `joe_announce_arrival` sets `guest_mode_announced` (before announcing, see the JK-1251 follow-up below) so the generic "guest mode enabled" line is skipped (same block as the other guests). PR #166 had put him directly under `all_people` to avoid this; reverted. A new person goes in `family`, `extended_family` or `guests`; `tests/test_people_groups_membership.py` fails if a referenced `person.*` is in none, unless it is in the test's `EXCLUDED` list (the kids, who have no trackers). Reload: groups (`group.reload`) and automations (`automation.reload`).
- **Joe is gated on iPhone detect like the parents (JK-1251 follow-up, 2026-11-14).** `joe_home`, `joe_announce_arrival` (held 1 min), the garage unlock and `guest_mode_on` need `device_tracker.joe_iphone_detect`. Recorder: detect had no false home in a week and one real visit UniFi never saw; UniFi led it by 25 s on one arrival and trailed it by 10 s on another, and lingered 6 min after he left. `joe_announce_arrival` sets `guest_mode_announced` BEFORE announcing: `guest_mode_on` checks the flag 2 min after he counts, and the hold plus the announcement shrinks that margin. The parents still set it after announcing (same exposure; not changed).
- **A quick action needs a listener that outlives the push (JK-1257, 2026-11-15).** The critical medication escalation sent no `actions`, so there was nothing to tap, and the reminder's ids (`TAKEN_<run context>`) are heard only by that run's `wait_for_trigger` for 15 minutes. The escalation now sends fixed ids (`NINO_MEDICATION_TAKEN_<dose>`) handled by `nino_medication_push_action.yaml`, which marks the dose taken and clears the push by tag. Both medication pushes open `home#notifications` (the header bell's popup). `tests/test_nino_medication_push_action.py` fails if a sent action id has no handler. Reload: automations.
- **Fixed ids are gated on "outstanding now", never on "escalated" alone (JK-1259, 2026-11-15).** The reminder's Taken button sent `TAKEN_<run context>`, so an older reminder's tap was dropped. It now sends `NINO_MEDICATION_TAKEN_<dose>`, the same id as the critical push; `nino_medication_push_action.yaml` acts while the dose is pending or escalated and not taken and not skipped (a leftover push from yesterday does nothing until that dose is outstanding again today; the reminder push is only cleared on a push tap or a skip, so a button or Alexa mark leaves it tappable, BACKLOG). The reminder wait no longer hears a taken tap; it ends on the boolean flipping. The taken script's fallback takes dose 2 only once it is due, pending, or dose 1 has been given: with dose 1 skipped, an early Alexa phrase would otherwise mark dose 2. Reload: automations, scripts.
- **Skip is its own boolean, and every "is this dose open?" reader must learn it (JK-1168, 2026-11-15).** `nino_medication_<n>_skipped` leaves `_taken` off, so supply (which triggers on `_taken` off to on) is untouched. The readers are the reminder (conditions, the four in-run guards, the wait), the pending-dose sensor, `resolved_dose`, both escalation branches, and the popup cards. Test every one in the positive form `not is_state(..., 'on')`, so a missing helper keeps the reminder and the backstop firing. The Skip push action uses a fixed id (`NINO_MEDICATION_SKIP_<dose>`) gated on "pending or escalated today". Dose 1's time is `sensor.nino_medication_1_effective_time` in the trigger, the pending sensor and the escalation; a consumer left on the raw helper fires early on no-school days. Reload: automations, template entities and input_boolean/input_datetime (no restart).
